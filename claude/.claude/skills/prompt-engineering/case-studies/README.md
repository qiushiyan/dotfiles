# Case studies

Each file here is one past pass in one domain: what a model-facing text got
wrong, what changed, and what the change measured. The rulebook's body
stays general and lean. A case study holds the domain half that only
sessions working in that domain need, so most sessions never load it. You
are here to add one, or to judge whether a pass deserves one.

**When a pass earns a case study.** It taught something the body cannot hold
because only one kind of work needs it, and it has evidence: a failure seen
from the model's side, and a measured before and after. Its general lesson
graduates into `../SKILL.md`; the case study keeps the specifics and points
back to it. A pass with no domain half gets a body change or an
`EVIDENCE.md` line instead.

**Name it for the search that should find it.** A future session searches
for its topic, not for your project. Lead the file name and the title with
the general topic (the kind of model or text), then the domain:
`classifier-judge-questions-jev-steward.md`, titled "classifier and judge
questions — the steward's Jev judgments".

**Write it semi-transferable.** Organise it around the questions a writer in
a neighbouring domain faces, not around your code change. Keep the domain's
paths and numbers as evidence for each point, not as the point. Close with
three answers:
- what transfers, and to which readers;
- what does not, pointing back to the body;
- what belongs to a vendor or project, pointing to its owner.

**Done when:**
- the opening says when to read it and when not to;
- `../SKILL.md` § Pointers names it by its trigger, in one line;
- `../EVIDENCE.md` records what was checked against the source.

This repository is public, so leave out customer text and credentials.
