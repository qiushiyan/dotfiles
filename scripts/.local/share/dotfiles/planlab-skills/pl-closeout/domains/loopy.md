# Loopy

Loopy serves from the app and the Loopy master. The app's release promotes
the master when a promote is owed, so ship it as [app.md](app.md) says; a
master change no app release carries ships with `pl-deploy-loopy-master`.

The production work is the team's Loopy closeout,
`.agents/skills/pl-loopy-handoff-distill/SKILL.md` in the checkout. Read its
§ Where Loopy's state lives, then § The owed reads, § The production
records, § The announcement, and § What people read for anything a person
will read. Its rules hold, announcements' included, with one difference:
this closeout is the invocation that approves `record-causes --commit` for
the causes this merge shipped.

Two brief readings are Loopy's: a brief whose whole goal was a production
read is deleted once that read is a posted verdict or a marker in its record;
a brief that lost a strand to this merge, or that `brief check` reports over
budget, is rewritten to what remains.
