# Handoffs

Run the brief sweep's closeout, `~/.claude/skills/distill-handoffs/SKILL.md`
§ Closeout steps 1 to 5, on the PR's head branch as the check prints it.
Apply its verdicts as § What waits there allows, and report each as a default
with its undo. Loopy's two brief readings are in
[domains/loopy.md](domains/loopy.md).

When the merge leaves accepted work that no brief holds, write one by the
repository's handoff guide (`.agents/skills/pl-loopy-handoff/HANDOFF.md` for
Loopy, `~/.claude/skills/handoff/SKILL.md` otherwise). The user redoes one
they would have written differently.

What to start next is the sweep's step 7: the brief this work hands to, else
the first unstarted brief in its domain, else that nothing queued follows.
